**What to build:** The layout survives pages where YouTube puts something other than related videos in the same column the Comment Pane lives in — most commonly the playlist panel on a radio playlist.

**Blocked by:** 01: The Comment Pane appears beside the Player

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] On a radio playlist, where YouTube places a playlist panel in the same column as the Comment Pane, the layout still works.
- [ ] The playlist panel is not displaced, hidden, or broken by the extension.
- [ ] The Comment Pane does not overlap or collide with another occupant of that column.
